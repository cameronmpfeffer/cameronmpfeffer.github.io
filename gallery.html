SN 2025aedb field viewer → I’m currently investigating an anomalous supernova called SN2025aedb. This supernova has strong hydrogen features, indicating it is the aftermath of a massive star collapsing. The oddity is that this supernova happened in the middle of nowhere! It’s floating in intergalactic space, where there should be no massive star populations. Take a look at this viewer which shows archival images of the field as well as my own observations. Can you find the host galaxy? Me neither! Best on a desktop browser · about 45 MB
